04 — Brand voice
Authoritative
Speak with confidence rooted in data and results. Cite outcomes, not opinions. Lead with insight, not questions.
Accessible
No jargon for its own sake. Translate complex SEO and marketing concepts into plain, compelling language decision-makers can act on.
Strategically warm
Professional but never cold. Write like a trusted advisor — direct, honest, genuinely invested in the client's growth.
Results-led
Every piece of content answers "so what?" Lead with the outcome: traffic, conversions, rankings. Numbers create credibility.
✓ Do say
"Increased organic traffic 30% in 3 months"
"No fluff — just strategies that rank and convert"
"Let me show you what's possible"
✗ Don't say
"I'm passionate about content"
"Synergizing your brand presence"
"Guru / ninja / rockstar"

08 — Graphics & imagery
Photography style
Clean, well-lit lifestyle and workspace imagery. Desaturate slightly and add a warm emerald or navy tint overlay at 15–20% opacity to unify stock photos with brand palette.
Graphic elements
Use geometric line accents (thin 1px emerald lines, diagonal dividers, dot grids). No clipart. No rainbow gradients. Halftone or grain textures allowed sparingly.
Layout principle
Generous white/ivory space. Left-aligned type. Gold horizontal rules or left-border accents to separate sections. One dominant visual per layout.
09 — Motion & animation
Motion personality
Confident and smooth — never frantic or flashy. Elements enter from left or bottom (reading direction). Use ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits.
Timing standards
Micro-interactions: 200–300ms · Element transitions: 400–600ms · Section reveals: 600–800ms · Logo animation: 800–1200ms
Video templates
Intro (~2s): Logo fades in → wordmark slides right → gold accent draws
Outro (~3s): Fade to navy → logo centered → CTA + socials
Lower third: Emerald bar slides left → name + title fade in
Never use: Star wipes, 3D flips, bouncy/elastic easing, excessive particle effects, or spinning transitions.

11 — Channel tone guide
LinkedIn
80% Authoritative, 20% Warm. Thought-leadership posts, data-backed insights, strategic takes. Lead with specific results and frameworks.
Twitter / X
60% Results-led, 40% Accessible. Punchy insights, quick tips, thread-worthy breakdowns. Short sentences. Numbers front-loaded.
YouTube / Video
50% Warm, 50% Authoritative. Conversational mentor delivery, data on screen, personal anecdotes. Teach like a trusted advisor.
Instagram
60% Accessible, 40% Results-led. Visual-first carousels, quick-win tips, behind-the-work process. Relatable but expert.
Voice mission: "I speak like a knowledgeable mentor who's genuinely invested in your growth — not a vendor pitching services."
