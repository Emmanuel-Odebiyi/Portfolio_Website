04 — Brand voice
Authoritative
Speak with confidence rooted in data and results. Cite outcomes, not opinions. Lead with insight, not questions.
Accessible
No jargon for its own sake. Translate complex SEO and marketing concepts into plain, compelling language decision-makers can act on.
Strategically warm
Professional but never cold. Write like a trusted advisor — direct, honest, genuinely invested in the client's growth.
Results-led
Every piece of content answers "so what?" Lead with the outcome: traffic, conversions, rankings. Numbers create credibility.
✓ Do say
"Increased organic traffic 30% in 3 months"
"No fluff — just strategies that rank and convert"
"Let me show you what's possible"
✗ Don't say
"I'm passionate about content"
"Synergizing your brand presence"
"Guru / ninja / rockstar"

08 — Graphics & imagery
Photography style
Clean, well-lit lifestyle and workspace imagery. Desaturate slightly and add a warm teal or navy tint overlay at 15–20% opacity to unify stock photos with brand palette.
Graphic elements
Use geometric line accents (thin 1px teal lines, diagonal dividers, dot grids). No clipart. No rainbow gradients. Halftone or grain textures allowed sparingly.
Layout principle
Generous white/ivory space. Left-aligned type. Gold horizontal rules or left-border accents to separate sections. One dominant visual per layout.
