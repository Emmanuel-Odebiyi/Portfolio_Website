How-To Guide

Applying Your DTF Sticker Print

01

Position

Place your garment (t-shirt, hoodie, or bag) on a flat, hard, heat-resistant surface. Preheat the area for 5 seconds to remove moisture and wrinkles, then position the DTF transfer film exactly where you want it, with the design face up.

02

Press

Set your household iron to **Medium-High (Wool/Cotton)** heat (approx. 150°C-160°C / 300°F-320°F) with **no steam**. Place a sheet of parchment paper or a thin clean cloth over the film, then press the iron down firmly with heavy pressure for 12 to 15 seconds.

03

Peel

Allow the garment to cool completely to room temperature (about 2 minutes). Once fully cold, slowly and gently peel the transparent carrier film from one corner. If any part of the design lifts, lay it back down and iron again.

Is the DTF print washable? +
Yes! Our DTF prints are made from professional, highly elastic plastisol-based inks. When applied correctly following our iron-on guide, the print will easily stretch without cracking and withstand up to 50+ wash cycles. For maximum durability, wash the garment inside out and avoid direct ironing on the printed area.
