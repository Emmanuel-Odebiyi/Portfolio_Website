About Makarios Worship

Makarios Worship is a gathering of believers inspired by the Holy Spirit to create an atmosphere of true worship, spiritual awakening, and revival — particularly among students, young professionals, and the youth community.

Now in its 4th edition, Makarios Worship 4.0 is centred on the theme PENTECOST — a call to experience a fresh outpouring of God's presence and power.

Partner with us to make this worship experience impactful and transformative for an entire generation.
